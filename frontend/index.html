    Fair questions.

    Are the quotes real?

    Yes. Every quote is fetched from its source during the scan and links to the original. Gemini only refers to evidence by ID, and the backend drops any ID it cannot find.

    Is the score written by an AI?

    No. The Viability Score and the Trajectory come from fixed formulas over measured signals such as the Trends slope, the share of 1★ reviews and competitor ratings. Each factor shows its working.

    What if my idea has no app?

    For B2B SaaS, D2C brands or offline businesses, You.com web research supplies the competitors, complaints and coverage instead of the Play Store.

    Which sources does it read?

    Play Store reviews, Google Trends, Google News, Reddit and Google Autocomplete through SerpApi, plus web and news search through You.com.

    Is it built for India?

    Yes. Trends use geo=IN, reviews come from the Indian Play Store, and numbers are formatted the Indian way.

    What does AI-suggested mean?

    No live post supports that claim, so treat it as a hypothesis. It is shown with a dashed border and never carries a quote.
